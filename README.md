### [AI Highlight Clip](https://github.com/toki-plus/ai-highlight-clip)

**问题 | Problem：** 数小时的长视频里只有几分钟值得分发，人工通看一遍的成本太高。
Hours of long-form video contain only minutes worth distributing, and watching everything manually is prohibitively slow.

AI 辅助的长视频初筛工作流：Whisper 转写、结构化评分、时间轴定位与 FFmpeg 处理相结合，输出候选高光片段供人工终审。

An AI-assisted triage workflow that turns long-form video into candidate clips through transcription, structured scoring, timeline selection, and FFmpeg processing — with human review as the final gate.

**Focus:** unstructured-content processing, human review points, and end-to-end workflow design.

### [AI TTV Workflow](https://github.com/toki-plus/ai-ttv-workflow)

**问题 | Problem：** 把一篇文案做成配音、字幕、封面齐全的短视频，需要在多个工具间反复切换。
Turning a script into a finished short video — voiceover, subtitles, cover — means repetitive switching between several tools.

连接文案准备、语音合成、字幕生成、画面配置与渲染的桌面工作流，关键节点保留人工确认。

A desktop text-to-video workflow connecting copy preparation, speech synthesis, subtitle generation, visual configuration, and rendering, with human-in-the-loop checkpoints.

**Focus:** service boundaries, repeatable production steps, and human-in-the-loop automation.

### [Auto USPS Tracker](https://github.com/toki-plus/auto-usps-tracker)

**问题 | Problem：** 跨境电商团队每天要在 USPS 页面手工逐单查询几百个运单号，再整理成报表。
Cross-border e-commerce teams hand-check hundreds of tracking numbers on the USPS site every day, then compile reports manually.

批量物流状态采集、异常处理、数据规范化与 Excel 报告自动化的脱敏案例，查询频率控制在合规范围内。

A sanitized case study of batch shipment-status collection, exception handling, normalization, and Excel reporting, with compliant query pacing.

**Focus:** business-process automation, data quality, external-service reliability, and operational reporting.

### [Video Mover](https://github.com/toki-plus/video-mover)

**问题 | Problem：** 同一内容要适配并发布到多个平台，素材处理、文案与排期全靠人工拼接。
Adapting and publishing the same content across multiple platforms means manual stitching of media processing, metadata, and scheduling.

可配置的内容运营工作流：文件监听、媒体处理、元数据生成、定时调度与多平台适配器组合为一条流水线。

A configurable content-operations workflow combining file monitoring, media processing, metadata generation, scheduling, and platform adapters.

**Focus:** workflow decomposition, cross-system integration, task orchestration, and operational boundaries.
